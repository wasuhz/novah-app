Novah privacy policy

Last updated: November 9, 2026

What Novah uses from your Google account

  • Your email address, to show which account is signed in on the TV.
  • Novah's own hidden folder in your Google Drive (the app data folder), to store a backup of your Novah settings. Novah cannot see, open or change any other file in your Drive.

What is stored

Your Novah settings: favorites, channel groups, preferences and the TV service sign-in. The backup is encrypted on the TV before it is saved to your Drive.

What is not done

  • Your data is not sent to the developer or anyone else, and is not sold or shared.
  • No advertising, tracking or analytics.

Removing your data

Choose Sign out in Novah's settings, and remove Novah's access at myaccount.google.com/permissions. To delete the saved backup, open Google Drive on the web, go to Settings, Manage apps, find Novah and choose "Delete hidden app data".

Google API data

Novah's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
